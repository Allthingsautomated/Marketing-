Our Story

From a One-Van Electrical Business to a Full-Service Automation Agency

AllThings Automated started in 2015 when founder Jamie Watts spotted a gap in the market: clients wanted one trusted company to handle their electrical work and their smart-home tech — not two separate contractors who didn't speak the same language.

Over ten years, we expanded from domestic rewires to enterprise network deployments, workflow automation platforms, and beyond. Today, our team of 30+ engineers, installers, and developers delivers projects from smart residential villas to multi-site corporate infrastructure.

One thing hasn't changed: we still treat every project like it's our own home or business. No cutting corners, no passing the buck, no vanishing after the invoice is paid.
